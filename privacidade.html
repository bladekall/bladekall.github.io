Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

▶ This application uses YouTube API Services

Automacao Canais ("the app") uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos on the YouTube channels owned by the app's owner. By using the app, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Information accessed

  • youtube.upload: permission to upload videos and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: reading the authorized channel's own uploads, only to check which publish dates are already scheduled.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channels.
  • The only API data stored is the video ID and scheduled publish date of videos the owner uploaded. No personal data of any other user is collected.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted (GitHub Actions encrypted secrets) and are never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.
  • The app does not use cookies and does not place or read any information on users' devices or browsers.

Data retention and deletion

  • Stored API data (video IDs and publish dates) is kept only while needed for scheduling and is automatically deleted no later than 7 days after the video is published, and never kept for more than 30 days without being refreshed.
  • Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page. After revocation, the stored token stops working immediately and is removed from the app's encrypted secrets within 7 days.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, contact the owner (below); data is deleted within 7 days of the request.

Contact

Through the GitHub profile github.com/bladekall.

Política de Privacidade (Português)

O aplicativo Automacao Canais usa os Serviços de API do YouTube para enviar e agendar vídeos nos canais do próprio proprietário. Ao usá-lo, você concorda com os Termos de Serviço do YouTube; aplica-se também a Política de Privacidade do Google.

São armazenados apenas os IDs e as datas de publicação dos vídeos enviados pelo proprietário. Os tokens ficam criptografados e nunca são compartilhados. Nenhum dado é vendido ou repassado a terceiros, e o app não usa cookies.

O acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento nas configurações de segurança do Google. Os IDs e datas armazenados são apagados automaticamente até 7 dias após a publicação do vídeo; tokens revogados são removidos em até 7 dias; qualquer dado é excluído em até 7 dias mediante pedido.
